Fangorn Explorer

Reading the app registry on Arbitrum Sepolia…

Every app here is read live from the Fangorn AppRegistry, and only apps whose agent cards verify on chain are listed. Ranking runs in your browser against each app's own published coverage, so nobody sees what you searched. Reputation is the ERC-8004 reputation registry's feedback for the app's agent. Agents: fangorn-mcp has the same ranking as find-apps, and this page registers find-fangorn-apps and describe-fangorn-app over WebMCP.
